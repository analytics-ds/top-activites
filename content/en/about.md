## Our mission

Top Activités is an independent editorial media dedicated to group outings and activities in Paris. The goal is to help each reader find the right option at the right time, whether for a private occasion or a corporate event.

There are countless ways to bring a group together in Paris, from action games to leisure bars, escape games, urban treasure hunts and unusual venues. Choosing the right one calls for clear, factual and comparable information.

## What you will find here

- Rankings and selections for each occasion (team building, hen and stag parties, birthdays, afterwork)
- Comparisons between venues and activities, citing several options to stay neutral
- Practical guides on budget, group size and season
- Answers to the questions people ask before booking

## Our independence

No sponsored content, no commercial affiliation. Selections are made on the merit of venues and activities. When a venue is recommended, competitors are mentioned too, so readers can decide for themselves.
