"What are you doing for your birthday?" The question that always comes too late, when every Saturday slot is already gone. To spare you yet another restaurant where half the table does not talk, we gathered 12 birthday ideas in Paris, sorted by what your group wants to experience, each with a budget benchmark and a link to our detailed selection.

## Start with three questions

Before looking for an idea, answer these three questions, they rule out half the options.

| Question | If the answer is… | Go for… |
|---|---|---|
| How many guests? | Fewer than 8 | Room, booth or table for a small group |
| | 8 to 30 | Activity with staggered starts, several booths |
| | More than 30 | Full venue hire |
| What budget per person? | Under 15 € | Board game bar, karaoke, drinks at home |
| | 15 to 35 € | One hour unusual activity |
| | 35 € and up | All-inclusive package with cake |
| What do you feel like? | Moving | Action game, axe throwing |
| | Chatting | Board game bar, dinner, activity bar |
| | Being surprised | Escape game, murder mystery, virtual reality |

If you are looking for a venue first, our guide on [where to celebrate your birthday in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/where-to-celebrate-birthday-paris/) compares six venues with birthday packages.

## Ideas to get moving

**1. An action game.** One hour of team challenges, physical and brainy, then a bomb to defuse. It is the best idea for a group that wants to burn off energy, from age 7, and birthday packages start at around 30 euros per person. Everything is explained in our guide to the [action game in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/action-game-paris/).

**2. Axe throwing.** The idea that gets people talking the most, between 19 and 27 euros per person depending on the venue and the number of players per target. Perfect for a small group of two or three per lane.

**3. Team virtual reality.** Everyone discovers it at the same time, nobody has a head start. Count around 30 euros per person for 45 minutes of adventure, a bit more with a birthday package.

These three ideas and four more are detailed in our selection of [original birthday ideas in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/original-birthday-ideas-paris/).

## Ideas to chat and toast

**4. An activity bar.** A booth of your own, a game, a drink and food without changing venues. A shooting bar, for example, starts at 16 euros per person for 1h30. It is the formula that best keeps an adult evening going.

**5. A board game bar.** The gentlest and cheapest idea, 3 to 5 euros per person to access the game library, drinks extra. Ideal for a birthday with 6 or 8 people. Our favourite venues are in the selection of [board game bars in Paris](/en/bars-lieux-ludiques/board-game-bars-paris/).

**6. Private karaoke.** From 10 euros per person for two hours in a private room, up to 30 people. Unbeatable with a close group, less suited to a group that does not know each other.

**7. Dinner or drinks in a private space.** Many bars will reserve an area with no hire fee in exchange for a minimum spend, to be negotiated beforehand. It is the right option from 30 people, or when the birthday is first and foremost a meal.

For an adult birthday, our [adult birthday party in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/adult-birthday-party-paris/) guide compares seven activity bars and turnkey packages.

## Ideas to surprise

**8. A surprise party.** Book an activity for a small group with the birthday person, while the other guests are already waiting on site. Tell the venue when booking, most will play along and bring the cake out at the right moment.

**9. A murder mystery.** A live role-playing game where you have to unmask the culprit, often led by an actor. Count around 28 euros per person for one hour, with 6 to 8 players. Our piece on the [murder mystery party in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/murder-mystery-party-paris/) explains how a game works.

**10. A themed birthday.** Choose an activity that matches the birthday person's passion, a superhero-licensed escape game for a comics fan, a custom quiz with questions about their life for a group of old friends. The theme gives a thread for the decor, the cake and even the dress code.

## Ideas by age

**11. For a child or a teenager.** Go for an all-inclusive birthday package, activity then a reserved space for the party snack, between 30 and 50 euros per child. Check the minimum age and the age up to which an adult must stay, it ranges from 14 to 15 depending on the venue. It is all detailed in our [teen birthday party in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/teen-birthday-party-paris/) selection and our [12th birthday party in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/12th-birthday-party-paris/) guide.

**12. For a milestone birthday, 30, 40 or 50.** The group is bigger and more mixed than usual. Choose an activity anyone can do and a venue that can take everyone at once, several booths, staggered starts or full venue hire beyond 30 people.

## And if you would rather give than organise

Sometimes the best birthday idea is to let the birthday person pick their own date. A voucher for an activity to do with friends is lived with others and never ends up on a shelf. Our selection of [birthday gift ideas in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/experience-gift-ideas-paris/) compares six gift vouchers, with their price and validity period.

## The mistakes that ruin a birthday

Booking too late, first of all. For a Saturday, allow two to three weeks, a month during school holidays and in December. Choosing an activity that is too niche, next, which leaves half the group on the sidelines. And finally, forgetting what comes after, a one hour activity that ends at 8pm with no plan afterwards is an evening that stops dead. Always plan the drink or dinner that keeps it going.
