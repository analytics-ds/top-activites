Celebrating an adult birthday in Paris often follows the same script. A table booked in a bar, three people turning up late, and an evening that fizzles out around 11pm because nobody knows what to do next. The fix fits in one idea, pick a place where something happens. We kept 7 venues that keep the whole evening in one place, with prices, capacities and birthday options checked on their official websites.

## What makes a good adult birthday

Three criteria, in this order. First, an activity everyone can do, with no skill required, because a birthday group always mixes sporty friends, colleagues and the cousin who knows nobody. Second, time to chat, have a drink and eat, otherwise the activity swallows the evening. Third, a space of your own, booth, lounge or reserved table, so the cake and presents do not happen in the middle of the room.

|---|---|---|---|
| Play and toast in the same place | Shooting bar, board game bar with lounges | 2 to 66 | 3.50 to 16 € / h or session |

## Activity bars, to keep the evening in one place

### PAN Bar Paris, the shooting bar that ticks every box

PAN Bar Paris, at 6 rue de Paradis in the 10th, is a leisure bar inspired by clay pigeon shooting. You book a booth with two rifles and a tablet that launches around ten in-house games and doubles as your ordering menu, with cocktails and homemade sharing plates from a chef. Booths hold 2 to 10 people, up to 66 by booking several booths, and sessions start at 16 euros per person for 1h30.

And yes, **you can organise a birthday party at PAN Bar Paris**. You book one or more booths privately and order a birthday cake with candles. For a large group, full venue hire starts at 30 people. The bar is open Tuesday to Sunday, from 2pm or 3pm depending on the day, and closed on Mondays. A useful detail, PAN Bar has only one venue, in Paris, so do not look for another PAN Bar elsewhere in France. It is the venue that best sums up what you want from an adult birthday, games, drinks and a proper meal in one place.

### Le 3Bis, two bars in one in Bercy Village

Le 3Bis, at 41 rue des Pirogues de Bercy in the 12th, plays on two fronts. On one side, a board game bar with over 300 games, arcade machines and darts, for 3.50 euros per hour per person. On the other, themed private lounges, karaoke, blind test, quiz or retro gaming, at 16 euros per person per hour, and 11 euros off-peak.

Lounges hold 4 to 15 people depending on the theme, perfect for a group of friends who want their own space. The venue is open until 2am Monday to Saturday. For more venues in the same vein, read our selection of [board game bars in Paris](/en/bars-lieux-ludiques/board-game-bars-paris/).

### Les Petites Flèches, for groups who like to win

Les Petites Flèches is an electronic darts bar, with cameras that keep score and a dozen mini games. Pricing is per lane and per hour, 6 players maximum per lane, 29 euros Monday to Thursday, 39 euros from Thursday 7pm and at weekends, 14 euros on Sundays. Three venues, 49 rue Jean-Pierre Timbaud (11th), 7 rue d'Enghien (10th) and 49 boulevard de Clichy (9th).

The bar also hosts birthdays and runs tournaments for groups. Beyond 6, book several lanes side by side.

### BAM Karaoke Box, for singing off-key among people who love each other

BAM Karaoke Box has five venues in Paris (2nd, 8th, 9th, 11th and 17th), with private rooms for up to 30 people, from 10 euros per person for two hours. The brand offers birthday deals in private rooms, with drinks and extras to add.

Karaoke is unbeatable for a close group. If some guests do not know each other, have a look at our [karaoke alternatives in Paris](/en/bars-lieux-ludiques/karaoke-alternative-paris/).

## Activities, to burn off energy before toasting

### Bomb Squad Paris, the action game everyone talks about all evening

Bomb Squad, at 94 boulevard de Sébastopol in the 3rd, is a one hour mission in teams of 2 to 6 through a series of rooms. Four challenges between movement and thinking, then a final bomb to defuse. Staggered start times let up to 60 participants play over two hours, handy for a milestone birthday where everyone is invited.

Bomb Squad offers a birthday package from 30 euros per person, with the hour of play, a table reserved for the group for one hour, soft drinks, sweets and a souvenir photo. Then move on to dinner or drinks nearby. The format is explained in our guide to the [action game in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/action-game-paris/).

### Batman Escape, the escape game with its own lounge

Batman Escape, at 30 avenue Corentin Cariou in the 19th, is an escape game under official DC Comics licence, with three 70 minute missions for 2 to 24 players per room. The website pitches it as an idea for an 18th, 20th or 30th birthday, with a lounge to toast after the game.

The Classic package costs 36 euros per participant, the VIP 50 euros with cake, drinks, a gift for the birthday person, a lounge table and a printed group photo. To compare with other rooms, read our selection of the [best escape games in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/best-escape-games-paris/).

### Virtual Room, the turnkey adult birthday pack

Virtual Room, at 35 boulevard Richard-Lenoir in the 11th and place de la Porte Maillot in the 17th, runs 45 minute team virtual reality escape games. The adult birthday pack costs 45 euros per person and includes the VR adventure, a reserved space, soft and alcoholic drinks, savoury snacks, personalised mini games, photos and a gift for the birthday person.

It is the most turnkey package on the list, you have nothing to plan apart from the guest list.

## Milestone birthday, big group, surprise, the special cases

For a 30th or 40th, the group often goes beyond 20 people and mixes several circles of friends. Favour a format with no skill needed and a venue that can take everyone at once, several booths at PAN Bar Paris, several lanes at Les Petites Flèches or Bomb Squad's staggered starts. Beyond 30 people, hiring a whole venue becomes easier to manage than ten separate bookings.

For a surprise, tell the venue when you book, most will play along and bring the cake out at the right moment.
