Laser tag and bowling are a bit like the kids menu of birthday parties. They work, everyone knows them, but nobody talks about them the next day. The good news is that Paris has a proper collection of unusual activities that keep everything that makes those classics work, team play, no skill needed, one hour flat, with the bonus "wait, what is this" effect. We kept 7, with prices, capacities and minimum ages checked on their official websites.

## The right reflex, replace rather than reinvent

Laser tag and bowling work for three reasons. Everyone gets the rules in two minutes, you play as a team or against each other, and it fits into an hour. A good alternative keeps those three ingredients and only changes the experience. The table below gives you the ideal replacement depending on what your group likes about the classic.

| Your group likes… | The classic | The original alternative | Budget per person |
|---|---|---|---|
| Running, aiming, burning energy | Laser tag | Action game | From 30 € as a package |
| Playing with a drink in hand | Bowling | Shooting bar | From 16 € (1h30) |
| Pure competition | Bowling | Axe throwing | 19 to 27 € |
| Laughing and teasing | Karaoke | TV-style quiz | 24 to 26 € (1h) |
| Solving a case | Escape game | Murder mystery with an actor | From 28 € |
| Trying something never seen | Laser tag | Team virtual reality | 30 € |

## To get moving, the alternatives to laser tag

### Bomb Squad Paris, the action game that makes you forget laser tag

Bomb Squad, at 94 boulevard de Sébastopol in the 3rd, is a one hour mission in teams of 2 to 6 through a series of rooms. Floor tiles, lasers to dodge, memory and teamwork challenges, then a final bomb to defuse. You keep the physical side of laser tag and add brains and a real storyline. It is open from age 7 with an adult up to age 15, and staggered start times let up to 60 participants play over two hours.

Bomb Squad offers a birthday package from 30 euros per person, with the hour of play, a table reserved for the group for one hour, soft drinks, sweets and a souvenir photo. To understand the format, read our guide to the [action game in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/action-game-paris/).

### Virtual Room, team virtual reality

Virtual Room, at 35 boulevard Richard-Lenoir in the 11th and place de la Porte Maillot in the 17th, runs 45 minute virtual reality escape games played as a team. The standard rate is 30 euros per person, 26 euros for under 26s. For a birthday, the kids pack costs 40 euros from age 8 with a party snack, the adult pack 45 euros with drinks and snacks.

The advantage of VR is that nobody has a head start, everyone discovers it at the same time.

## To raise a glass, the alternatives to bowling

### PAN Bar Paris, the shooting bar that replaces bowling

PAN Bar Paris, at 6 rue de Paradis in the 10th, uses exactly the same mechanics as bowling, one booth per group, taking turns, scores on screen, but with two rifles and around ten in-house shooting games instead of pins. The booth tablet is also used to order cocktails and homemade sharing plates. Booths hold 2 to 10 people, up to 66 by booking several, from 16 euros per person for 1h30.

For a birthday, you book one or more booths privately and order a cake with candles. The bar is closed on Mondays. For more ideas in the same spirit, read our guide to [alternatives to a classic bar in Paris](/en/bars-lieux-ludiques/alternative-to-a-classic-bar-paris/).

### Quiz Room, the TV game show for real

Quiz Room is a TV game show set with buzzers, in a private room for your group. Four venues in Paris, Bonne Nouvelle (10th), Odéon (6th), Opéra (9th) and La Villette (19th), for 3 to 54 people. Count 26 euros per person for one hour with 3 to 6 players, 24 euros with 7 to 18, and around 33 to 35 euros for 1h30 and two or three games.

It is open from age 14, with a Kids version from age 8, and you can add custom questions about the birthday person, the option that gets the biggest laughs at a party.

## For the wow effect, the truly unusual activities

### Wildness, axe throwing in the 13th

Wildness, at 79 rue de Patay in the 13th, offers axe throwing, with shurikens, crucifix or shovel as options. Pricing is per lane, 54 euros for two (27 euros per person) and 66 euros for three (22 euros per person), plus 5 euros per lane for the options. The venue is open Tuesday to Friday from 5pm to 10pm, Saturday from 2pm to 10pm and Sunday from 2pm to 7pm.

It is the activity that makes the biggest impression in conversation, and the lane format for two or three players suits a small group of friends well.

### Les Cognées, axes and archery in the 18th

Les Cognées, at 5 rue Stephenson in the 18th, also offers axe throwing, from 19 to 27 euros per person depending on the number of players per target, and archery from 25 to 35 euros. The venue is open Friday from 4pm to 10pm, Saturday from 11am to 10pm and Sunday from 2pm to 6pm.

It is the cheapest option on the list when there are several of you per target.

### Cinévasion, the murder mystery with a real actor

Cinévasion, in the 15th, runs a one hour introductory murder mystery led by a professional actor, for 6 to 8 players, from 28 euros per person. The venue also has escape games, karaoke and a cinema room, with a private party room as an option. Our piece on the [murder mystery party in Paris](/en/activites-insolites/murder-mystery-party-paris/) explains how a game works.

## Choosing without getting it wrong

Three questions are enough. Who is the youngest in the group, because the minimum age quickly rules out some options. How many of you there are, because a murder mystery caps at 8 while the action game or the quiz go beyond 50. And whether the group wants to eat and drink on site, in which case an activity bar like PAN Bar Paris saves you changing venues.

For booking, allow two to three weeks for a Saturday, a month during school holidays.
