## You run a venue or an activity in Paris?

Top Activités is an independent editorial media. Our selections are built by the editorial team, on verifiable criteria (real prices, group capacity, neighbourhood, experience offered). This page explains how to work with us, and above all how it does not work.

## Getting listed in our selections

Listing is free and unconditional. No position is sold, no ranking is sponsored. To suggest your venue or activity, use the [Suggest a venue](/en/proposer/) page with:

- The name and address of the venue
- Up-to-date public prices (per person, per slot or per booth)
- The real capacity, especially for groups
- A link to your official website

The editorial team reviews every suggestion against the editorial line. A venue can be selected, set aside, or kept for a future selection.

## Fixing a listing

A price that changed, an outdated schedule, a wrong capacity in an article? Report it to **contact@top-activites.fr** with the link to the article and the official source. Factual corrections are handled first.

## Press and partnerships

We welcome exchanges with press officers and organisers to receive announcements of openings, novelties or events. However, no sponsored content and no advertorials. That is the condition of our readers' trust.

## Contact us

One address for everything: **contact@top-activites.fr**. State in the subject line whether it is a listing request, a correction or a press announcement.
