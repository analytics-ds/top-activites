Planning a friend's bachelorette party comes down to three unknowns at once, the budget the group can actually put together, the activity that will land without putting anyone on the spot, and the timing so the best slots aren't already gone. Paris has the advantage of packing a huge range of activities within half an hour of each other, which makes the logistics of a multi-part day much simpler. Here is how to set the budget, pick the right format for the vibe you want, and time the calendar so the bride only has to sit back and be surprised.

## How much does a bachelorette party cost in Paris

The budget varies hugely depending on whether the day stays in Paris or turns into a weekend with accommodation. For a single-day format, budget between 50 and 150 euros per participant, activity, meal and small extras included. For a weekend with an overnight stay, the range climbs to between 200 and 450 euros per person, with lodging alone accounting for 50 to 150 euros depending on the standard chosen.

| Format | Budget per person | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| Single activity | 20 to 50 euros | One activity, meal not included |
| Full day in Paris | 50 to 150 euros | Activity, meal, extras |
| Weekend with accommodation | 200 to 450 euros | Lodging, activities, meals |

The rule that comes up most often among maids of honour who organise these, the bride pays nothing or almost nothing, and the rest of the group splits the bill evenly. Sharing the total budget from the invitation onward avoids nasty surprises and last-minute drop-outs, especially once the group passes ten people.

## Which activity to pick depending on the vibe you want

Picking the right activity depends less on the current trend than on the bride's real personality and the group joining her. Three questions almost always settle it, does she prefer laughing or relaxing, does she like being in the spotlight or would she rather stay part of the group, and does the day stop after the activity or carry on into the evening.

**For a group that wants to move and laugh without putting anyone on the spot**, an [action game like the one at Bomb Squad](/en/activites-insolites/action-game-paris/) ticks every box. Teams of 2 to 6 players run through a course of challenges over an hour, a format that works just as well for 6 friends as for a larger group of 20 thanks to staggered starts.

**For a calmer moment with a view over the rooftops**, head instead to a [Paris rooftop bar](/en/bars-lieux-ludiques/rooftop-bars-paris/) for drinks, then pair it with dinner in the same neighbourhood to cut down on travel.

**For an evening that has to end in songs**, private karaoke remains a safe bet, with a room closed off just for the group, away from the eyes of a public bar. Our [alternative to classic karaoke](/en/bars-lieux-ludiques/karaoke-alternative-paris/) breaks down the formats available for a group that wants more than a single mic at the back of a bar.

**For a group that enjoys thinking together**, an [escape room](/en/activites-insolites/best-escape-games-paris/) remains a classic that works, as long as you keep each room to 2 or 6 players and book several rooms in parallel for a larger group.

## Other formats that always land well

Beyond thrill-seeking activities, a handful of formats come up again and again in bachelorette parties that go well, without needing to book a specific venue months ahead.

**Brunch with the girls** remains the safest way to open the day, generally costing between 25 and 40 euros per person for a full package with drinks. It is the moment that settles the group before the activity, without the stress of a tight schedule first thing in the morning.

**A spa or beer spa** suits groups that want a relaxing pause before a more intense evening activity, usually costing between 40 and 70 euros per person for a two-hour session.

**A treasure hunt around Paris** shows off a neighbourhood in a different way, solving clues as a team on a smartphone, for a budget that rarely goes above 20 to 25 euros per person. It is a good option for groups who like walking and want a daytime activity without booking an indoor venue in advance.

**A rage room**, where you smash crockery and furniture in a space built for it, appeals to groups looking to burn off wedding-planning stress with a bat in hand. Sessions usually cost between 30 and 50 euros per person depending on the length and the number of items to smash.

## Day, evening or weekend, how to decide

Three formats cover most bachelorette parties organised in Paris, each with very different budget and scheduling constraints.

| Format | Length | Budget per person | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single afternoon or evening | One 2 to 3 hour slot | 50 to 100 euros | A group short on time or budget |
| Full day | Activity, meal and evening out | 100 to 150 euros | A group that wants to mark the occasion without staying overnight |
| Weekend | Two days with accommodation | 200 to 450 euros | A group with more time and a bigger budget |

A weekend sounds great on paper, but it means syncing everyone's availability, which gets complicated past around ten participants. A packed day in Paris, activity in the afternoon then dinner and drinks in the evening, covers the same goal without the scheduling constraints or the cost of a trip.

## Who organises it and when to book

By tradition, the maid of honour takes charge of the organising, alone or with a few close friends if several people are especially invested. The rule that shapes everything else, the programme is built around what will please the bride, not around the tastes of whoever is organising.

On timing, start planning between two and six months before the wedding, the window where the bride is not yet swamped by final wedding preparations and guests still have time to block their date. For the activities themselves, book four to eight weeks ahead in normal periods, and up to three months if the party falls between May and September, Paris's peak season, when the best Saturday slots go first.

## Two concrete venues to round out the programme

Two Paris venues cover the two most requested vibes for a bachelorette party well, pure action on one side, a shared moment over a game on the other.

[Bomb Squad](/en/activites-insolites/action-game-paris/), on boulevard de Sébastopol in the 3rd arrondissement, runs a one-hour action game course in teams of 2 to 6 players, open from age 7 with an adult. The sliding price drops from 45 euros per player for two down to 28 euros for six, and staggered starts let up to 60 people play in the same afternoon, useful if the party brings together several friend circles who don't all know each other.

PAN Bar, in the 10th arrondissement, works as a virtual shooting bar starting at 16 euros per person, in booths of 2 to 10 players. For a larger group, several booths can be booked in parallel for up to 66 players, and a full buyout of the venue's 400 square metres starts at 30 people. The bar is closed on Mondays, worth keeping in mind if the party falls early in the week.
