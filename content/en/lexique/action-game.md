An action game is an immersive course made of several challenge rooms, both physical and mental, that your team chains together to score as many points as possible. The clearest picture: an urban Fort Boyard. Unlike an escape game, you are not trying to get out of a single room, you move through a series of timed challenges where everyone finds a role, the agile and the brainy alike.

## In practice

- **Duration**: from about one hour to two hours depending on the venue, briefing included.
- **Team size**: challenges are played in small teams, and large groups rotate as several teams in parallel with a final leaderboard, which makes it a great format for team building, bachelor and bachelorette parties.
- **Physical effort**: moderate and adjustable; most venues mix dexterity, logic and agility challenges so everyone takes part.

## Where to play an action game in Paris?

The offer is narrow, which is what makes the question tricky. Venues fall into two categories.

- **In central Paris**, Bomb Squad runs this format on boulevard de Sébastopol in the 3rd.
- **On the outskirts**, large complexes such as Koezio (Cergy, Sénart) run it on bigger surfaces, with longer courses but a journey to plan.

Not to be confused with laser tag or indoor paintball, which are built on teams facing off rather than on a scored challenge course.

## Action game or escape game, what is the difference?

The [escape game](/en/lexique/escape-game/) works your brain in a single room; the action game adds body and movement across a course. For a group craving competition and energy, the action game wins.
