A karaoke box is a soundproof private room your group rents by the hour to sing without an audience, Japanese style. Unlike bar karaoke where you perform on stage in front of the whole room, here only your friends hear you butcher your favourite song. The box includes screens, microphones, the song catalogue and, in most venues, drinks service straight to the room.

## In practice

- **Pricing**: per box and per hour, not per person. Rates vary by room size, day and time, with weekday slots noticeably cheaper than Saturday night.
- **Group size**: from small rooms for 4 or 5 up to large boxes. In Paris, BAM Karaoké Box rooms go up to 30 people, for example.
- **Booking**: essential on weekends, evening rooms go first. On weekdays, last-minute slots are easier to find.
- **Best occasions**: bachelor and bachelorette parties first, then birthdays and team afterworks that want a big finish.

## The right reflex

Always check the room's real capacity before booking. A box for 10 with 15 people crammed in guarantees a night spent standing without a microphone.
