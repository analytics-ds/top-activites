A team building event is an organised moment outside the usual work setting that brings a team together around a shared activity, to strengthen cohesion, communication and mutual understanding. What sets it apart from a simple outing with colleagues is that it pursues an identified goal, for instance onboarding new hires, bonding two departments that rarely talk, or marking the end of an intense period.

## In practice

- **Duration**: from one hour to a full day. One or two hours of activity are enough to restart a team dynamic, as long as you keep informal time afterwards.
- **Budget**: expect roughly 25 to 40 euros per person for a standalone activity, 50 to 80 euros with a meal or cocktail, and 100+ euros for a full day with privatisation.
- **When to book**: as early as possible for a weekday evening slot. In November and December, the peak season, several weeks in advance are necessary.

## Team building or seminar?

A seminar is offsite work time (presentations, workshops, strategy) that may include a team building session. Team building itself is centred on the activity and the group, with no mandatory work content. Many companies combine both in a single day.
