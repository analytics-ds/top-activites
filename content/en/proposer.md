## Suggest a venue or activity

Top Activités lists the best group outings and activities in Paris. A venue, an activity or a concept deserves to appear in our selections? It can be flagged to the editorial team.

Please include:

- The name of the venue or activity
- The neighbourhood or address in Paris
- The type of occasion (team building, bachelor party, bachelorette party, birthday, afterwork, night out with friends)
- A link to the official website

Every suggestion is reviewed case by case against our editorial criteria. Listing is free and unconditional: no position is ever sold.

Write to us at **contact@top-activites.fr**.
